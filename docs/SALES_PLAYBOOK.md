## 1. Core Positioning & Value Proposition

### The One-Sentence Pitch
> "I am an independent visibility and accountability layer between the biller and the owner. I don't replace anyone. I don't require changing systems. I tell the owner what's actually happening with their money."

### What N2N Is NOT
- Not a billing company
- Not a practice management system replacement
- Not a clearinghouse
- Not a coding service

### What N2N IS
An **intelligence layer** that sits on top of whatever systems the practice already uses:
- Ingests data from Trizetto (clearinghouse), Ethizo (PM/EMR), and bank statements
- Extracts structured claim data from EOB PDFs using AI
- Surfaces the answers that billers bury in spreadsheets — or never report at all
- Gives the owner independent, real-time visibility into their own revenue cycle

### Why "Independent" Matters
Every metric a biller reports was produced by the biller. There is no independent check. Owners have no way to verify the numbers they receive are accurate, complete, or timely. N2N is the independent audit layer — not accusatory, but factual.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile

### Primary Target
- **Independent medical practices** with 1–5 physicians
- **Revenue:** $500K–$5M/year billed
- **Pain:** Uses a third-party biller (no internal billing staff)
- **Situation:** Owner is a clinician, not a financial expert — relies entirely on biller's reporting

### Secondary Target
- Multi-location practices with centralized billing
- Practices that recently changed billers and suspect data was left behind
- Practices under payer contract renegotiation (need clean data)
- Practices adding a new physician or specialty (need forecasting)

### Disqualifying Signals
- Practice does billing entirely in-house with a dedicated billing manager who is engaged and knowledgeable
- Very small practices (<$200K/year billed) — ROI math doesn't work
- Practices unwilling to share data access (required for onboarding)

## 4. The Five Discovery Questions

These five questions open the door. If the owner **cannot answer them**, you have proven your value before the demo begins.

> **Rule:** Never answer these questions for them. Let silence do the work.

### Question 1 — Clean Claim Rate
> "What is your clean claim rate?"

**Benchmark:** 95%+ is industry standard. Below 90% is a problem.

**If they don't know:**
> "A clean claim rate below 95% means more than 1 in 20 claims requires rework before it gets paid. That's a direct labor and cash flow cost. Do you know if your biller is tracking this?"

### Question 2 — Days in AR
> "What is your average days in AR?"

**Benchmark:** <40 days is healthy. >60 days signals a cash flow problem.

**If they don't know:**
> "Days in AR is how long it takes from when you see a patient to when you collect payment. If you don't know this number, you don't know how fast your revenue cycle is moving."

### Question 3 — Denial Rate & Top Denial Codes
> "What is your denial rate, and what are your top three denial codes?"

**Benchmark:** <5% denial rate. Top codes should be known and actively managed.

**If they don't know:**
> "Denial patterns repeat. If you don't know your top denial codes, the same errors are happening every month and costing you the same money every month."

### Question 4 — Contracted Rate Verification
> "How do you verify that you're being paid correctly against your contracted rates?"

**Expected answer:** "I trust my biller."

**Response:**
> "Most owners do. But payers regularly underpay against contract — especially after rate updates or modifier changes. Without an independent check, short payments accumulate silently."

### Question 5 — Payer-Specific History
> "What happened to your [specific payer — e.g., Aetna / Medicare] claims last quarter?"

**If they can't answer:**
> "That's exactly the problem. You see aggregate numbers, but you can't drill into a specific payer and ask: how many claims, how much billed, how much paid, what was denied, and why? N2N answers that question in seconds."

## 5. Three Budget-Opening Pain Points

### Pain Point 1 — "I don't trust my biller"
**The real fear:** "What if my biller is missing things, or worse, covering things up?"

**How to open:**
> "You don't need to accuse anyone of anything. You just need independent data. N2N gives you a second set of eyes — automated, impartial, always on."

**What N2N delivers:** Cross-reference ERA data against your PM system charges. Surface claims that were created in your system but never sent to the clearinghouse. Flag short payments against contracted rates.

### Pain Point 2 — "I'm working harder but collecting the same"
**The real fear:** "We're seeing more patients but revenue isn't growing."

**How to open:**
> "There are only three reasons revenue doesn't grow with volume: charge capture failure, denial leakage, or write-off creep. N2N tells you which one — and where."

**What N2N delivers:** Charge capture rate (claims created in PM vs. claims billed to payers). Denial trend over time. Write-off audit by type and authorization.

### Pain Point 3 — "I can't afford [new physician / expansion / equipment]"
**The real fear:** "I don't actually know what I'm collecting or what I could collect."

**How to open:**
> "Before you can plan for growth, you need to know your baseline. What's your current collections rate? What's your reimbursement per visit? What would happen to cash flow if you added 50 patients a week? N2N builds that forecast from your actual data."

**What N2N delivers:** Collections timeline analysis. Revenue per CPT code. Projection modeling using actual reimbursement rates.

## 6. The RCM Iceberg

> **The damage is done before the biller ever sees the claim.**

Most owners blame the biller when claims get denied. But the root causes are almost always upstream:

```
VISIBLE (what owners see)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Denied claims
  Short payments
  Slow AR
────────────────────────────────────────────────
HIDDEN (where the damage actually happens)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Front desk: incorrect insurance at check-in
  Registration: wrong subscriber ID, wrong DOB
  Insurance verification: not done before visit
  Authorization: missing prior auth for procedure
  Documentation: provider didn't support medical necessity
  Coding: upcoded, downcoded, or wrong modifier
  Charge capture: visit created in PM, never billed
────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

**The biller gets blamed for problems they didn't create.**

**Positioning implication:** N2N doesn't just surface what the biller missed. It helps the owner understand where in the revenue cycle money is being lost — so they can fix the process, not just the symptom.

## 7. Key Insight — Intelligence, Not Data

> **This is the most important concept in the sales process. Lead with it in all marketing.**

### The Problem with "Data"
Every billing system, clearinghouse, and PM software gives the owner data:
- Aging reports
- Payment summaries
- Claim status screens

**But data is not answers.** Owners are clinicians. They don't have time to build pivot tables or interpret EDI files. When they get a 200-row denial report, they don't know what to do with it.

### What N2N Provides: Answers
- "Your Aetna denial rate jumped 40% last quarter. The top code is CO-4 — missing modifier. That's $18,000 sitting in denied claims."
- "You have 23 charges created in Ethizo that were never sent to Trizetto. That's $11,400 that was never billed."
- "Your average submission lag increased from 4 days to 9 days last month. That's cash flow impact."

### The Intelligence Layer
> "Practices don't have an AI problem. They have a visibility problem."

The dashboard, the drill-down, the timeline analysis, the exception flagging — **this is the product.** The AI extraction is the plumbing. The intelligence layer is what owners pay for.

**Never lead with "we use AI to extract EOBs."** Lead with: "We tell you where your money is going."

## 8. Objection Handling

### "My biller handles all of this."
> "Your biller reports on performance. But who audits the auditor? There's no independent check on whether the numbers you receive are complete, accurate, or timely. N2N is that check — it doesn't replace your biller, it verifies them."

### "I don't have time to learn another system."
> "The dashboard is built for a clinician — not a billing specialist. You log in, you see your numbers, you get answers. The complexity is handled by the system. Your job is to read the results."

### "This seems expensive."
> "Compared to what? A healthcare CFO costs $15,000 to $25,000 a month. A fractional CFO costs $3,000 to $8,000. N2N delivers the same financial oversight, automated, for a fraction of that. And if we find even one month of missed claims or underpayments, the system pays for itself."

### "I trust my biller."
> "Trust is a fine starting point. Verification is better. You trust your bank, but you still check your statement. N2N is the statement for your revenue cycle."

### "We're not ready right now."
> "Understood. Can I ask — is there a specific trigger that would make this a priority? New physician joining? Payer contract renewal? End of fiscal year? Most practices that say 'not yet' come back six months later when something surfaces. The data gap is already costing you — it's just not visible yet."

### "We're thinking about switching billers."
> "That's actually the best time to start. Before you switch, you want a clean baseline — what's the AR balance, what's pending, what was written off, what claims are in dispute. When you switch billers, all of that context can disappear. N2N captures it."

## 10. Demo Script

### Opening (2 min)
Start with the five discovery questions. Don't demo until you've identified their pain.

### Dashboard Overview (3 min)
> "This is your executive summary. Everything on this page comes from your actual data — your ERA files, your EOBs, your bank statements. There's no manual data entry. It updates automatically."

Walk through:
- Total AR balance
- Claims submitted this month
- Denial rate (flagged if >5%)
- Days in AR trend

### Drill Into a Denial (3 min)
> "Let's click into denials. Here's your denial breakdown by payer, by code, by dollar amount. See this? CO-4 is your top denial code. That means missing or incorrect modifier. Every claim with CO-4 can be corrected and resubmitted. This is money sitting in denied status waiting to be collected."

### Black Hole Detector (2 min)
> "This is the most important page. These are charges that were created in your billing system — meaning you saw the patient, the visit was documented — but the claim was never sent to the clearinghouse. It's not denied. It's not pending. It just doesn't exist in the payer's system. This is revenue that has completely fallen through the cracks."

### Closing (2 min)
> "What you're looking at is an independent view of your revenue cycle — not filtered through your biller's reports, not summarized in a way that hides problems. This is your money. Now you can see it."
